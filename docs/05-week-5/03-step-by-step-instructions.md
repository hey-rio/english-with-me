# Bài 3 — Hướng dẫn từng bước

## Từ nối cực quan trọng

- First — đầu tiên
- Then — sau đó
- Next — tiếp theo
- After that — sau đó
- Finally — cuối cùng

## Mẫu hướng dẫn

> First, open Settings.

> Then, go to Network.

> Next, select Wi-Fi.

> After that, reconnect to the network.

> Finally, try again.

## Flow IT Support mẫu

> First, close Zoiper.

> Then, check your internet connection.

> Next, open Zoiper again.

> After that, make a test call.

> Let me know if it works.

## Câu kiểm tra kết quả

- Does it work now?
- Can you hear me now?
- Can you log in now?
- Do you see the error?
- Is it working now?
