# Week 5 — Practice

## A. Sắp xếp câu

Sắp xếp:

- send me the ID
- open TeamViewer
- wait for the connection
- find your ID

### Đáp án mẫu

1. First, open TeamViewer.
2. Then, find your ID.
3. Next, send me the ID.
4. Finally, wait for the connection.

## B. Dịch

1. Bạn cần restart service.
2. Hãy thử dùng mạng khác.
3. Đầu tiên, mở Settings.
4. Sau đó, kiểm tra Wi-Fi.
5. Bây giờ nó hoạt động chưa?

### Đáp án

1. You need to restart the service.
2. Try to use a different network.
3. First, open Settings.
4. Then, check Wi-Fi.
5. Is it working now?

## C. Role-play

User:

> I can't connect to the VPN.

Support cần nói ít nhất 4 câu hướng dẫn.

### Đáp án mẫu

> First, check your internet connection. Then, disconnect the VPN. After that, connect again. If it still doesn't work, try to restart the VPN application.
