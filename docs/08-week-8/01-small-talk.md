# Bài 1 — Small Talk cơ bản

Bạn không cần nói chuyện sâu. Chỉ cần vài flow quen thuộc.

## Chào hỏi

> Hi, how are you?

> I'm good, thanks. How about you?

## Công việc

> What do you do?

> I'm a system engineer.

> I work with servers, networks, and VoIP systems.

## Hỏi ngày làm việc

> How's your day going?

> Pretty good. It's a bit busy today.

## Hỏi cuối tuần

> How was your weekend?

> It was good. I stayed home and relaxed.

## Khi không biết nói gì

- That's nice.
- I see.
- Really?
- Sounds good.
- Nice to hear that.

## Giới thiệu bản thân mẫu

> Hi, I'm Hao. I'm a system engineer. I work with infrastructure, servers, networks, and contact center systems. I also work with VoIP and monitoring tools.

Không cần cố nói dài hơn khả năng.
