# Week 7 — Role-play

Không nhìn đáp án trước.

## Case 1 — VPN

User:

> Hi, my VPN isn't connecting.

Bạn cần:
- hỏi internet
- hỏi error
- hỏi đã restart app chưa
- yêu cầu restart
- yêu cầu thử lại
- xác nhận kết quả

### Mẫu tham khảo

> Got it. Is your internet connection working?

> Do you see any error message?

> Did you restart the VPN application?

> Okay. Can you restart it now?

> Please try to connect again.

> Is it working now?
